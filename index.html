What I'm

Impact of Forest Fires on Global Air Quality

Analyzing PM2.5 concentrations and aerosol optical depth using NASA FIRMS satellite data to understand how wildfire smoke alters air quality across regions.

→ Remote Sensing · Data Analysis
⚛️

Quantum Computing Project @ QNepal

Active contributor to quantum computing research and initiatives at QNepal — exploring algorithms, quantum gates, and bridging theory with implementation.

→ Quantum Computing · Algorithms
⚡

Lightning Data Analysis & Visualization

Time series analysis of lightning events, peak current exploration, and 2D/3D geographic visualizations to study atmospheric discharge patterns over Nepal.

→ Data Science · Geophysics
💉

Blood Pressure Study — Green Plant School

Led research analyzing blood pressure variations across age groups (students, staff, teachers) — an early exercise in empirical data collection and medical research.

→ Public Health · Research Methods

→ AI · Web Development
